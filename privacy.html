PoolDrive – Privacy Policy

PoolDrive is a personal tool that shows several of the owner's own Google Drive accounts as one combined drive, and a companion backup script that uploads the owner's PC folders to those accounts.

What data is accessed

With your permission, PoolDrive reads and manages files in the Google Drive accounts you sign in with: listing folders and files, showing storage space, uploading, renaming, moving and trashing files you choose.

Where data goes

PoolDrive has no server. Everything runs in your own browser or on your own PC. Your files and sign-in tokens go only between your device and Google. Nothing is sent to, stored by, or shared with the developer or any third party.

Storage on your device

Sign-in tokens and settings are kept in your browser's local storage (web app) or in a local settings file (backup script). You can remove them at any time by removing the account in PoolDrive, clearing site data, or deleting the files, and you can revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of Google user data

PoolDrive's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the file features above, never for advertising, and is not transferred to anyone.
